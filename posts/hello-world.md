# Hello, World!

This is the first post on my blog. Consider this a test of the markdown rendering engine.

## Why a blog?

I wanted a lightweight way to share thoughts — no CMS, no build step, just `.md` files and `git push`.

## What can it render?

Pretty much everything you'd expect from markdown:

- **Bold text** and *italic text*
- [Links](https://github.com/3xela)
- Lists (like this one)
